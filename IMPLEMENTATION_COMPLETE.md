## 📊 Project Summary

### What Was Built
A complete, production-ready **NestJS backend** for a real-time chat and messaging platform called **TalkBus** with support for:

- ✅ **1:1 Direct Messages** - Private conversations between users
- ✅ **Group Chat Rooms** - Multi-user group messaging with membership
- ✅ **Community Talk Posts** - Public posts and announcements
- ✅ **User Authentication** - Guest login, email/password, SMS verification
- ✅ **Real-time WebSocket** - Live messaging via Socket.io
- ✅ **User Profiles** - Profile management with preferences
- ✅ **Online Status** - Real-time presence tracking
